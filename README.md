# Lausanne M1 Metro — Handrail Redesign

**EPFL · Bachelor's Final Project · February–May 2026 · Team of 2**  
**Industrial partner: Transports Lausannois (TL)**

**Mechanical Design · CAD & FEA · Railway Engineering · Industrial Collaboration**

## Overview

A pre-industrial engineering study conducted with Transports Lausannois (TL) as our final Bachelor's project at EPFL. We proposed the project after identifying standing areas with insufficient handholds on Lausanne's M1 metro.

Working in a team of two, we studied the existing vehicle fleet and developed two complementary handrail concepts for potential deployment across the 22-unit fleet. The project progressed from problem identification and concept evaluation to engineering verification and technical documentation.

## Engineering Approach

- **Fleet assessment:** Reviewed the existing handrail arrangements and constraints across three generations of M1 vehicles.
- **Benchmarking:** Studied comparable metro handrail solutions and assessed candidate materials and manufacturing methods.
- **Concept development:** Compared three design concepts and developed a floor-to-ceiling pole and a suspended handrail.
- **Mechanical analysis:** Performed finite element simulations and preliminary checks of connections and fatigue behavior.
- **Requirements:** Assessed applicable railway engineering, fire safety and accessibility standards.
- **Industrial preparation:** Researched suppliers and prepared a preliminary cost assessment, assembly documentation, bills of materials and fabrication drawings.

**Tools:** Fusion 360 · Autodesk Inventor · Finite Element Analysis  
**Standards considered:** EN 12663 · EN 45545-2 · EN 16116 · EN 16584 · EN 16586 · Swiss DE-OCF / LHand
